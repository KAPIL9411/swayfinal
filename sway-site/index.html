FAQ

Straight answers.

Is SWAY really anonymous?

On the wall, yes — posts are not linked to your handle or email. For safety, reports can be traced to an account by the team. That trace is never visible to students.

My college isn’t listed. What now?

SWAY adds campuses in batches. New colleges are verified before they go live — you can request yours from inside the app.

Can faculty join?

No. Only student email domains are allowed, and faculty accounts are blocked on sight.

Will an iPhone home-screen app get updates?

Yes. The iPhone version is a web app that caches itself — open it with internet once and it picks up new versions automatically.

What does it cost?

Nothing. SWAY is free for students.
